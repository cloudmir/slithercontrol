## Verified implementation scope
- Independent 32px topological exit search, followed by executable turning/speed/continuous collision checks; entry manoeuvre can join a corridor behind current turning circle. Static guide and time-checked prefix are distinct.
- Hold selected route below V10_SWITCH_RISK; invalid/completed paths may change immediately. All accepted candidates carry numbered RISK labels. RISK is a heuristic index, not probability.
- Pellet approach continuously re-aims, verifies return to escape corridor, and preserves local target. Food waypoint requires continuing exit. Center preference is attenuated when food exists.
- V10 observed-command response lag changed 0.17→0.06 s after held-out fits on two runs. This is not server RTT.
- Current enemy target heading is recorded from client wang, predicted until reached, then straight. Future heading changes remain unknown; uncertainty padding retained.
- Final boost change: existing wrap/corridor/intercept classifications remove boost consumption penalty during escape; same-corridor faster boost is preferred only after passing path checks and no material increase (>0.02) in closure index. Food approach compares arrival time and retains existing mass/distance/alignment gates.

- Adjustable V10_BOOST_COST slider: default0, range0–1. Normal/food boost penalty changes with this setting; wrap/corridor/intercept escape remains exempt, explicitly stated in UI. Local0/1 test and browser change-persist-restore are separately recorded.
